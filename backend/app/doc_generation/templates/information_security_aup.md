# Information Security Policy and Acceptable Use

## Purpose and scope
Define acceptable use of organization systems, accounts, networks, data, and devices.

## User responsibilities
Set expectations for account protection, approved use, software installation, and reporting.

## Prohibited activity
Describe misuse, unauthorized access, circumvention, and unapproved disclosure.

## Monitoring and privacy
Explain monitoring boundaries and applicable notice requirements without making legal claims.

## Enforcement and exceptions
Define consistent handling, exception approval, and escalation.

## Acknowledgement and review
Set acknowledgement expectations and policy review cadence.
