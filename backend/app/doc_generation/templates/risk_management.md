# Risk Management Policy

## Purpose and scope
Describe the systems, business activities, and decisions covered by risk management.

## Roles and governance
Identify risk owners, reviewers, escalation paths, and decision authority.

## Risk assessment method
Describe how risks are identified, assessed, recorded, and prioritized; leave scoring choices for confirmation.

## Treatment and acceptance
Explain mitigation plans, accountable owners, approval of residual risk, and due dates.

## Monitoring and reporting
Set expectations for reassessment, material changes, overdue actions, and leadership reporting.

## Exceptions and review
Document exception approval and policy review cadence.
