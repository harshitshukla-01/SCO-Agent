# Data Management Policy

## Purpose and scope
Describe information covered across collection, use, storage, sharing, and disposal.

## Data ownership and classification
Define data owners and how sensitivity and handling requirements are assigned.

## Collection and permitted use
Limit collection to documented business purposes and identify approval responsibilities.

## Storage, access, and sharing
Set requirements for authorized access, approved locations, transfers, and third parties.

## Retention and deletion
Describe retention schedules, legal holds, deletion, and verification; use confirmed facts only.

## Exceptions and review
Document exception approval, incident escalation, and periodic review.
