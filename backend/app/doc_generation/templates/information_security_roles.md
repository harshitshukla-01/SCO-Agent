# Information Security Roles and Responsibilities

## Purpose and scope
Define the organizational activities and personnel covered by security governance.

## Governance and accountability
Describe leadership oversight, security coordination, risk ownership, and escalation.

## Role definitions
Set responsibilities for executives, policy owners, administrators, workers, and suppliers.

## Separation and conflicts
Describe how incompatible duties and conflicts are identified and managed.

## Communication and acknowledgement
Define how responsibilities are communicated and understood.

## Review and updates
Set ownership and cadence for keeping role assignments current.
