# Third-Party Management Policy

## Purpose and scope
Define suppliers, service providers, and other external parties covered.

## Ownership and risk classification
Assign business owners and describe how the organization assesses supplier risk.

## Due diligence and approval
Describe checks performed before access, data sharing, or contract approval.

## Contract and access safeguards
Set expectations for confidentiality, security terms, least privilege, and data return.

## Ongoing monitoring and offboarding
Describe periodic reviews, incident coordination, access removal, and relationship closure.

## Exceptions and review
Define documented exceptions and a review cadence.
