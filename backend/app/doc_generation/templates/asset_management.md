# Asset Management Policy

## Purpose and scope
Define information, software, devices, cloud resources, and other assets covered.

## Asset ownership and inventory
Specify accountable owners and the minimum inventory attributes to maintain.

## Classification and acceptable handling
Describe classification, approved use, access, storage, transfer, and protection expectations.

## Assignment, maintenance, and return
Cover allocation, configuration, repair, reassignment, and return of assets.

## Disposal and verification
Describe secure sanitization or disposal and evidence retained.

## Exceptions and review
Define ownership of exceptions and inventory review cadence.
