# Physical Security Policy

## Purpose and scope
Identify facilities, equipment, visitors, and work arrangements covered.

## Facility access
Describe authorization, credential handling, visitor records, and access review.

## Workspace and equipment protection
Set expectations for unattended devices, clear work areas, secure storage, and reporting loss.

## Environmental and facility safeguards
Define responsibilities for protecting equipment from environmental and utility risks.

## Remote work
Describe physical safeguards for equipment and information outside controlled offices.

## Exceptions and review
Document exception approval and review cadence.
