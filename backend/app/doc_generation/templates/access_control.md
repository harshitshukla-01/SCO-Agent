# Access Control Policy

## Purpose and scope
Define identities, systems, data, and administrative interfaces governed by this policy.

## Roles and responsibilities
Assign identity administration, resource ownership, approval, and review duties.

## Authentication requirements
Specify approved authentication methods, credential protection, and additional verification requirements. Mark deployment-specific choices for confirmation.

## Authorization and least privilege
Require access based on job need, approved roles, and documented authorization.

## Provisioning and access reviews
Describe request approval, timely provisioning, periodic review cadence, and evidence of review.

## Revocation and role changes
Define access removal for termination, transfer, leave, and suspected compromise.

## Privileged access and exceptions
Set controls for administrative access and time-limited, approved exceptions.
