# Incident Response Plan

## Purpose and scope
Define security events and services, information, and people covered by response.

## Roles and activation
Assign incident coordination, technical response, leadership decisions, and escalation.

## Reporting and triage
Describe how personnel report suspected incidents, how reports are assessed, and how records are protected.

## Containment, investigation, and recovery
Set response stages, evidence handling, service restoration, and decision documentation.

## Communications and notification
Identify internal, customer, supplier, and external communication owners; mark missing contacts and timelines for confirmation.

## Post-incident review
Require lessons learned, action ownership, and follow-up tracking.

## Exercises and maintenance
Set plan testing and review cadence.
