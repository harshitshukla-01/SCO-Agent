# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan

## Purpose and scope
Identify services, dependencies, people, and locations covered by continuity planning.

## Roles and activation
Define incident leadership, decision authority, communications, and plan activation criteria.

## Impact and recovery priorities
Record business impact assumptions, service priorities, recovery objectives, and owners; mark unknown targets for confirmation.

## Response and restoration
Describe backup protection, restoration sequence, dependency coordination, and validation.

## Communications and alternate operations
Identify approved internal, customer, supplier, and incident communication channels.

## Exercises and maintenance
Set exercise cadence, evidence requirements, lessons learned, and plan review.
